ACT III

Workflow Reengineering

Bolting AI onto legacy workflows preserves the constraints AI was meant to remove. I redesign workflows around what AI can actually deliver.

EVERY REDESIGN ANSWERS THREE QUESTIONS
01

Which decisions move to the system?

Where AI's judgment is measurably better, faster, or cheaper — and the failure modes are tolerable and recoverable.

02

Which decisions stay human?

Where judgment, relationships, or accountability demand a person — kept human deliberately, not by inertia.

03

How does accountability change?

When the system decides, someone still owns the outcome. Roles, escalation, and oversight redrawn to match the new division of labor.

The unit of transformation is the workflow, not the tool.
